    06 · CASE STUDY · PROJECT MANAGER & CLIENT COMMUNICATION

    ARC Affiliates: five industries, one CRM.

    GOHIGHLEVEL · KEEP SCROLLING, THE ROW MOVES WITH YOU

    THE CHALLENGE

    One place for every lead.

    ARC Affiliates runs sub-accounts across five industries. Each business had its own sales process, lead sources and follow-up needs, but every team needed one place to capture, organize and track its leads and opportunities.

    I was the Project Manager and the client's main point of contact. I planned and coordinated delivery across every sub-account and handled all client communication.

    SUB-ACCOUNTS 01–02

    Life insurance & credit

    • CRM workflows for lead management, follow-ups and opportunity tracking.
    • Optimized day-to-day client operations inside the CRM.
    • Mapped the challenges of maintaining these systems and costed the ongoing requirements, so the client could plan ahead.

    SUB-ACCOUNT 03

    Event management

    • Connected external systems to the CRM.
    • Leads and opportunities now sync automatically, so the team tracks everything in one place.

    SUB-ACCOUNTS 04–05

    Spa / medical, life & health

    • CRM workflows developed for spa/medical agencies and life & health clients.
    • Each workflow adapted to that business's specific requirements.

    ACROSS ALL ACCOUNTS

    Pipelines & automations

    Pipelines and automations that follow each lead from first contact to the right sales or service stage.

    THE RESULT

    Work completed. Client happy.

    Client teams can track and manage their opportunities far more easily. The client confirmed that all requested work was completed successfully and gave positive feedback on the implementations.
