HR · Launched Jan 2026

MEPPS: Monthly Employee Performance Point System

I designed and launched MEPPS at LofiStack. Each month, rule breaches and rewards are recorded as points against each employee, and finance uses those points to calculate deductions and rewards. Everyone works to the same clear, written rules, which has helped the company prevent and resolve workplace conflicts fairly and consistently.

  2. 2ReviewTotals checked each month
  3. 3ApplyFinance turns points into deductions and rewards

Since launch

  • Fewer disputes
  • Fewer late arrivals
  • Rewards paid out to those who earned them

Case study · Client project

ARC Affiliates: multi-industry CRM delivery on GoHighLevel

My role
Project Manager & client communication
Platform
GoHighLevel
  • Life insurance
  • Credit
  • Event management
  • Spa / medical
  • Life & health

The challenge

ARC Affiliates runs sub-accounts across five industries. Each business had its own sales process, lead sources and follow-up needs, but every team needed one place to capture, organize and track its leads and opportunities.

My part

I was the Project Manager and the client's main point of contact. I planned and coordinated delivery across every sub-account and handled all client communication.

What we delivered

  • Life insurance & credit: CRM workflows for lead management, follow-ups and opportunity tracking. We also mapped the challenges of maintaining these systems and costed the ongoing requirements, so the client could plan ahead.
  • Event management: external systems integrated with the CRM, so leads and opportunities sync automatically.
  • Spa / medical and life & health: workflows tailored to each business's own process.
  • Across all accounts: pipelines and automations that follow each lead from first contact to the right sales or service stage.

The result: client teams can track and manage their opportunities far more easily. The client confirmed that all requested work was completed successfully and gave positive feedback on the implementations.
